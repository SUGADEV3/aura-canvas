About

What Aura is, and isn't

Aura is a one-page tool for building a calm, personal visual: a gradient, a line of text, a bit of texture. Blend the colors, drop in a quote, and export the result as an image — for a phone wallpaper, a journal cover, a desktop background, or a quiet corner of a moodboard.

It started as a small experiment in making something with no real "output" beyond the way it looks. There's no template library to unlock, no account to make, and no gallery of other people's creations — just a canvas, a handful of controls, and whatever you decide to put on it.

How it works

Everything renders in your browser using the HTML canvas — the gradient, blur, grain, and text are all drawn locally on your device. Nothing you create is uploaded anywhere. Downloading or sharing an image happens entirely on your end; the tool has no server component to send it to.

Where it's going

The quote list and color presets get small additions over time based on what people reach for most. If there's a palette, font pairing, or feature you'd want to see, the contact link on the home page reaches a real person.
