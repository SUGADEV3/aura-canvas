About Aura

A small studio for making one nice thing

Aura is a free browser tool for building a calm, personal visual — pick a gradient, add a line of text, and export it as an image for a phone wallpaper, a journal cover, or a quiet corner of a moodboard. There's no account, no upload, and no tracking of what you make: every gradient is rendered locally in your browser and only leaves your device if you choose to download or share it. New quotes and presets are added occasionally based on what people find themselves reaching for.
